# Adidas US Sales Analysis – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze Adidas US sales and profitability performance across **time, products, retailers, regions, states, and sales channels**.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of key business KPIs and helps identify sales trends, high-performing products, profitable retailers, and regional performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall Adidas US sales and profitability.
* Track monthly sales performance and identify trends.
* Compare sales performance across regions and states.
* Identify top-performing products based on units sold and profit.
* Analyze retailer-wise profitability.
* Compare different sales methods such as **In-store, Online, and Outlet**.
* Build an interactive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – KPI and analytical measure development
* **Microsoft Excel** – Source dataset

## 💡 Key Insights

* Total Adidas US sales in the dataset are approximately **$899.9M**, generating around **$332.1M in total profit**.
* **July** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **$95M**, while **March** recorded the lowest at approximately **$57M**.
* **West Gear** generated the highest retailer profit at approximately **$86M**, followed by **Foot Locker ($81M)** and **Sports Direct ($74M)**.
* **Men's Street Footwear** is the leading product category in units sold, with approximately **4M units**, and also generates the highest product-level profit.
* The **West region** contributes the largest share of units sold at approximately **27%**.
* The dashboard enables users to interactively analyze performance by **year, month, region, product, retailer, state, and sales method**.
